# Psyambient

A spacious 70 BPM ambient rack that layers chaotic and filter-swept pads, a detuned wash, generative midrange melodies, and slowly evolving bass textures. Most voices are free-running; the pentatonic arpeggio alone locks to the shared clock. Each patch's sliders trade spectral weight, movement rate, and space against the calm underlying bed.

## Groups and controls

- **Atmosphere** (*Intensity*) holds the three layers; its slider moves each layer's own slider.
- **Soundscapes** (*Drift*): a group whose wash patch evolves (`Evolve(16)`), rotating its chorus and echo every 16 bars.
- **Mid Voices** (*Shimmer*): raises each voice's FM index.
- **Bass** (*Depth*): opens the sub filters and deepens the swell and rumble.
