# Low hat

## Sonic function

A low hat is a darker, less dominant hi-hat accent. It retains the short noisy
articulation of a hat while carrying more lower-mid body and usually appearing
less often in the pattern.

## Minimal architecture

trigger
→ noise or metallic source
→ less aggressive high-pass filtering
→ short amplitude envelope
→ output

## Tone

Compared with a bright hat, the filter leaves more of the source body intact.
This makes the voice feel closer, rounder, and less airy. It should still lose
enough low content to avoid competing directly with the kick and bass.

## Envelope

Keep the attack immediate and the decay short. A strongly exponential slope
keeps the accent percussive even when the spectrum is darker.

## What NOT to assume

Low hat does not mean an unfiltered low-frequency drum. It is still a hat voice:
its role is upper-rhythmic articulation, with a darker balance and typically a
sparser pattern.
