# Drums and Rhythm for Electronic Parts

Deeper theory: `../rhythm-groove/groove-and-feel.md` (swing, microtiming,

## Velocity and dynamics

The #1 fix for mechanical drums. Never use flat velocity.

```text
bad  (metronome): hat, step 0.25, amp .3 on every step
good (accented):  hat, step 0.25, amp .4 .15 .25 .15 .35 .15 .25 .1
```

### Accent principle
- **Downbeats** — strong hits
- **Offbeats** — ghost notes (amp 0.05–0.15)
- **Upbeats** — medium; they drive the groove forward

## Swing and shuffle

Express swing by alternating long–short step lengths:

| Feel | Step pattern (beats) |
|---|---|
| Straight 16ths | 0.25 0.25 … |
| Swung 16ths (≈60%) | 0.3 0.2 … |
| Heavy shuffle (triplet) | 1/3 1/6 … |

Accent the long note: swung hats with `step 0.3 0.2`, `amp .35 .15`.

## Ghost notes

Very quiet hits that fill gaps and make drums feel played:

```text
snare, step 0.25 (one bar of 16ths):
beat 1: -   -   -   -
beat 2: .5  .08 .06 .1     hit + ghosts
beat 3: -   -   .06 -      ghost only
beat 4: .5  .08 .1  .06    hit + ghosts
```

## Genre drum patterns

Tempos are typical ranges. Kick/snare positions are in beats of 4/4.

### House (120–128 BPM)
- Kick on every beat.
- Hats on the offbeats only: step 0.5, amp `0 .3 0 .25`.
- Clap on 2 and 4.

### Techno (128–140 BPM)
- Harder kick with syncopated variations: step lengths `1 1 .75 .25 1 1 .5 .5`.
- Driving 16th hats: amp `.4 .1 .25 .1`, alternating short/long decays
  (`.03 .02 .06 .02` s) for an open/closed feel.

### Hip-hop / boom-bap (85–95 BPM)
- Kick: step lengths `1.5 .5 1 1`.
- Snare on 2 and 4.
- Sparse swung hats: step `0.3 0.2`, amp `.2 .1`.

### DnB (170–175 BPM)
- Breakbeat kick: step lengths `1 .75 .25 .5 .5 1`.
- Snare on beat 3 of each half-time bar (not 2 and 4).
- Fast 16th hats: amp `.25 .08 .15 .08`.

### R&B (90–110 BPM)
- Kick: step lengths `1.5 1 .5 1`.
- Snare on 2 and 4, quieter (≈ .4).
- Rim clicks: step lengths `.75 .5 .75 (rest) .5 .5`, amp ≈ .2.
- Triplet-shuffled hats: step `1/3 1/6`, amp `.2 .08`.

### Garage / 2-step (130–135 BPM)
- Skippy kick: step lengths `.75 .5 .75 .5 .5 1`.
- Snare on beat 2 only.
- Shuffled hats.

## Hi-hats beyond straight 16ths

### Open/closed alternation
Vary decay per hit: `.02 .02 .08 .02` s (short–short–open–short).

### Accent placement by genre
- **House** — accent offbeats (pushes the groove forward)
- **Techno** — accent on-beats (driving, mechanical)
- **Hip-hop / R&B** — accent irregularly (human feel)

### Open hat on the "and" of 4
A classic tension builder at the end of a 2-bar phrase: keep closed decays
(0.02 s) everywhere, and on the 15th 16th of bar 2 raise amp to .4 and decay
to 0.1 s.
