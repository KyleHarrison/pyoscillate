## Notes / follow-ups

- The whole STRINGS section is implemented as Karplus-Strong strings (plucked: `patches/tonal/pluck/`, `bass/standup.py`; bowed: `patches/tonal/strings/bowed.py`) rather than as the subtractive recipes. The whole KEYBOARDS section is implemented in `patches/tonal/keys/` (struck FM, Karplus-Strong strings and held ranks) and the whole VOCALS section in `patches/tonal/vocal/` (formant voices and a whistle), also not as the recipes. The WOODWINDS section is implemented in `patches/tonal/woodwind/` as blown pipes on a shared `Wind` base (so far only the Flute, on a jet base `JetWind`; the reed, drone and lip winds are next), also not as the recipes. The PADS section is implemented in `patches/tonal/pad/` as the subtractive recipes themselves (two oscillators, noise, a 24 dB low-pass, one LFO) on a shared `SynthPad` held voice, each recipe's filter knob kept as its cookbook position; the filter stops at 10 kHz, where `MoogLP` is stable. The LEADS section is implemented in `patches/tonal/lead/`: `synth.py` holds the six recipes (subtractive, true-square pulses), and Brass Section and Mellow 70's Lead are `lead.py`'s older pulse leads. Every other section is extraction-only.
- One patch title on printed p.109 is visually clipped in the scan; title guessed as "Positronic Rhythm" from context (electronic/rhythmic SFX section) but should be verified against a cleaner scan before use.
- "Mono Solo" (p.92) ADSR values for filter/amp envelopes were partly obscured by a scan artifact (dark smudge over the R column) — re-verify against the source before implementing.
- Several patches reference book-specific LFO routing combinations (e.g. "PW osc1, osc2" as a single routing, dual-parameter LFOs) that don't map 1:1 onto a single Pyo LFO object — worth discussing during pyo-music/implementation translation.
